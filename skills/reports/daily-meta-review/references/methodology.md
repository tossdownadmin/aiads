# Daily review methodology

1. Establish the account currency, current status, dates and evidence availability. The trailing seven-day window sits inside the trailing thirty-day window: their totals are not independent before/after periods. Do not claim a trend from comparing those totals. Use dated ad series when a trend is supportable.
2. Assess spend and reported outcomes in the context of objective and optimization goal. Profile visits are not follower growth; conversations are not qualified leads; attributed purchases are not incremental sales. Missing values are unknown, not zero.
3. Prioritize current disabled/unsettled account states, delivery unexpectedly absent, conversion evidence gaps and objective-matched efficiency checks. Without an intended schedule or business target, state uncertainty rather than declaring underdelivery or failure.
4. Frequency of 3.5 or higher can prompt a warning and 5 or higher a closer review, but frequency alone never proves fatigue or saturation. Low CTR alone is not a critical problem for a video-view or awareness objective. CTR below 0.5% with at least 1,000 impressions is a review prompt only where destination traffic is relevant. These thresholds are this method's screening rubric, not universal platform benchmarks.
5. Purchase ROAS below 1 means reported attributed purchase value is below spend. It does not establish margin, net profitability, tracking accuracy or causality. Check attribution and order reconciliation before proposing action.
6. Describe creative trends only when individual ad IDs and comparable daily evidence are supplied. A preview or transcript excerpt does not mean the full image/video was examined. Do not infer audience or placement performance without breakdowns.
7. Sort findings by urgency. Use crit for substantiated material delivery/measurement concerns, warn for investigations and data gaps, ok for neutral observations or well-supported positive delivery. Do not assign a performance grade without a rubric.
8. Actions are human review recommendations. Never pause, change budgets or promise outcomes. Keep interpretations concise and avoid repeating raw metric numbers: cited evidence is expanded with exact application-calculated values in the report.

## Missing reporting rows

A missing account-level insight row is not a verified zero. Zero active campaigns at retrieval does not prove that no ads delivered earlier in a historical window. If spend/impressions are not reported, say that delivery cannot be established from the returned data. Never write “no ads were delivering” or “zero delivery” without explicit zero metrics for those dates. Distinguish source gaps, current paused states and verified historical zeros.
