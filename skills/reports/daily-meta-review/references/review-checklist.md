# Daily client review checklist

Review the following topics when evidence supports them. Do not force a finding for every topic or invent a positive just to fill a section.

1. **Results against the goal.** Match outcomes to optimization goals. Discuss purchases and attributed revenue for sales, leads and CPL for lead generation, Page/profile visits for visit campaigns, conversations for messaging, and reach or viewing outcomes for awareness/video. Compare only like outcomes. A cheap profile visit is not proof of higher business value than a video view.
2. **What changed.** `previous-window` is the immediately preceding nonoverlapping period of the same length. Match campaigns by ID. Use supplied previous/current metrics and application-calculated change facts. An absent previous row is unknown, not zero or a new launch. The current seven-day window sits inside thirty days; do not treat these as before/after. Account totals cover mixed objectives; prefer a campaign comparison for CPA/CPL.
3. **Budget pacing.** Only `budget-pacing` supplies a client-approved calendar-month budget and dated month-to-date spend. The linear projection assumes the observed daily average continues; schedules, promotions and seasonality can change it. Current daily/lifetime campaign and ad-set budgets are ceilings at retrieval, not historical approved spend commitments. Do not add campaign and ad-set budgets or infer past underdelivery from today's limits.
4. **Ads and creative.** Review all supplied ad facts. Identify measured concentration, contrasting ad efficiency within comparable objectives, and dated changes on the same ad ID. Daily frequency is not cumulative frequency and must not be summed. Sparse conversion counts require caution. A sustained CTR decline and rising CPC on comparable daily delivery can support a deterioration warning, but do not declare frequency the cause or guarantee that new creative restores performance. Ad copy and eligible asset formats can inform a proposed test; this text-only skill has not watched or visually inspected images/videos.
5. **Ad sets and funnel.** Use supplied ad-set outcomes and current goals, bids, schedule, audience and placement eligibility as context. Configuration is not performance by audience/placement. Do not diagnose which demographic/placement is losing money without breakdowns. Clicks, landing views, visits, conversations and purchases are different events and may have different attribution; treat a discrepancy as a reconciliation check, not a proven funnel leak. Notes supplied by a client do not turn Meta leads into verified qualified leads or conversations into bookings.
6. **Targets and business context.** `client-context` contains only explicit saved goals, targets and notes. Its currency must match the account. Apply a CPA/CPL/ROAS target only to the corresponding outcome and period. ROAS is attributed revenue/spend, not net profit or incremental return. No supplied margins, customer lifetime value or CRM record means profitability and lead qualification remain unverified. Never invent a target, offer or business type from a campaign name.
7. **Delivery and measurement.** Check dated spend, impressions, outcomes, returned rows and current account/inventory state separately. Use imported gaps honestly. A currently paused campaign can have substantial historical delivery. A missing field does not prove broken tracking or account restriction. Recommend checking the same dates, account timezone and attribution in Ads Manager before diagnosing a discrepancy.

Set category to `win` only for a supported positive outcome, `concern` for a measured performance concern, `data_gap` for unavailable measurements, and `observation` for neutral findings. Do not present lack of evidence as a win.

Write for the account manager: a short headline, what the observation means, one practical proposed action, and why it should be reviewed. `now` means a substantiated material concern for prompt human review; `next` means the next review/test cycle; `monitor` means follow-up or a measured win. Global action ordering is based on review priority, not a claim that unlike clients can be ranked by ROAS, CPL or currency.

For partial evidence batches, discuss supplied entities only. For final synthesis, consider every batch, merge repeated issues and retain both useful wins and material concerns. Candidate prose is a proposal; only cited measured facts can support the final findings.

## Quantitative text bindings

Never write literal numbers or spelled-out counts into prose. Use {{window0:campaign:EXACT_ID:spend}} (with an actually supplied ID, also cited in evidenceIds) when a scalar measurement belongs in the sentence. The application inserts its formatted value. For complex ad/ad-set facts, describe the qualitative observation and let the measured-support panel carry the values. Do not invent numeric targets, changes, CPA or lead counts. Campaign/ad names may be copied exactly as identity labels.
