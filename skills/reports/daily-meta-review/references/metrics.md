# Metric meanings and evidence use

Account totals are Meta account-level insights; reach is not summed across campaigns. Campaign metrics are scoped to the campaign and dates in each evidence ID. Spend uses the account currency. CTR is the reported percentage, not a fraction. ROAS is attributed purchase value divided by spend when values exist, or the platform-reported purchase ROAS. CPL/CPP/cost per visit are available only with matching positive outcome counts. Null means unavailable; explicit zero is distinct.

A campaign's current ACTIVE or PAUSED state and current account status describe retrieval time, not historical delivery for every day. A campaign may have contributed spend before it paused. Current budgets are ceilings, not spend; no pacing claim without dated budget history.

The evidence catalog includes dated account/campaign metrics, outcome classifications, availability notes, settings and bounded ad-level daily observations. Coverage notes disclose omitted ad details. Reference only supplied evidence IDs. Do not quote spend, CTR or outcome numbers from memory or another reporting window.
